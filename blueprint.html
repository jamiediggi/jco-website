Questions

Frequently asked questions

What if I can't implement it myself?

That's completely fine. The Blueprint works whether you implement it yourself, hire someone else, or work with my team. If you want help building, I'll recommend specific Build phases based on your priorities.

How is this different from other marketing audits?

Most audits tell you what's wrong. The Blueprint tells you what to do about it — in priority order. You get a complete customer journey strategy, not just a list of problems.

What if my business is different?

The Blueprint is built specifically for your business. It's not a template. Every strategy is tailored to your market, your customers, and your goals.

How long does it take?

The Blueprint is delivered within 1-2 weeks, including the walkthrough call. You'll have complete clarity on your marketing in under a fortnight.

What happens after the Blueprint?

You'll have everything you need to take action. If you want help implementing, I offer Build phases — done-for-you projects that bring your Blueprint to life. These typically range from £5,000-£15,000 depending on scope. But there's no obligation. The Blueprint stands alone.
