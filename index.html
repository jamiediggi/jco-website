The Framework

The Perfect Customer Journey

A simple 6-stage framework for marketing that works. Every successful business takes customers on a journey — whether they realise it or not.

1

Get Noticed

Get visible to the right people with a message that makes them pay attention.

2

Connect

Turn a moment of interest into an ongoing relationship with permission to follow up.

3

Nurture

Build trust and demonstrate value before you ever ask for a sale.

4

Convert

Make the sale feel like the obvious next step, not a hard sell.

5

Deliver & Wow

Exceed expectations and make them feel they made the best decision.

6

Create Fans

Turn happy customers into your best source of new business.
